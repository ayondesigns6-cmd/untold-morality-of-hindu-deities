Rape and Sexual Abuse

A structured reference catalog compiled from primary Sanskrit scriptures, canonical Puranas, and accredited academic and devotional translations (including WisdomLib, Vedabase, and Sacred-Texts).

Worst Verses Overview:

Rape: SB 8.12.26-33 by Shiva (2nd most popular Hindu god), SP 2.5 23.38-46 + BVP Prakriti Kanda 21.18-24 by Vishnu (most popular Hindu god), SkP 5.3.136.1 to 16 + BVP Krishna Janma Kanda 61.43-44 by Indra (also see Ramayana Balakanda Sarga 48 and 49 ), SB 9.20.36 + MP 49.17-28 by Brihaspati, BVP Krishna Janma Kanda 80.11-19 + SB 9.14.4 by Soma (Moon), SB 9.24.33 to 35 + DBP 2.6.13 to 35 by Surya (Sun)

Ramayana

Referenced in main summary: Balakanda Sarga 48 and Sarga 49 (The story of Indra deceiving and assaulting Ahalya).

Brihadaranyaka Upanishad

Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 6.4.7 (BU 6.4.7)

wisdomlib.org

सा चेदस्मै न दद्यात्, काममेनामवक्रिणीयात्; सा चेदस्मै नैव दद्यात्, काममेनां यष्ट्या वा पाणिना वोपहत्यातिक्रामेत्, इन्द्रियेण ते यशसा यश आदद इति; अयशा एव भवति ॥ ७ ॥

Wisdomlib Translation:

If she is not willing, he should buy her over; and if she is still unyielding, he should strike her with a stick or with the hand and proceed, uttering the following Mantra, ‘I take away your reputation,’ etc. She is then actually discredited.

Shiva Purana

wisdomlib.org

SP 2.5 23.38-46 (Vishnu and Vrinda)

38. On seeing her husband, Vṛndā too was delighted. She forgot her sorrow. She considered everything a dream.

39. Delighted in the heart and with all the dormant passions kindled up, she sported with him for many days in the middle of that forest.

40. Once at the end of the sexual intercourse she realised that it was Viṣṇu. Vṛndā rebuked him angrily and spoke thus.

41. Fie on this misdeed of Viṣṇu in outraging the modesty of another man’s wife. I have now realised you as the wielder of illusion, appearing in the guise of an ascetic.

42. Sanatkumāra said that she showed her powers as a staunch chaste lady by cursing Viṣṇu.

43. She called him a base foe and defiler of another person's virtue.

44. The two persons whom he made to appear before her would become Rākṣasas and abduct his wife.

45. He would be distressed due to separation from his wife and seek the help of monkeys in the forest.

46. After saying this, Vṛndā entered fire though prevented by Viṣṇu.

Bhagavata Purana / Srimad Bhagavatam

SB 8.12.26-33 (Shiva chasing Mohini)

सा तमायान्तमालोक्य विवस्त्रा व्रीडिता भृशम् । निलीयमाना वृक्षेषु हसन्ती नान्वतिष्ठत ॥ २६ ॥ तामन्वगच्छद् भगवान् भव: प्रमुषितेन्द्रिय: कामस्य च वशं नीत: करेणुमिव यूथप: ॥ २७ ॥ सोऽनुव्रज्यातिवेगेन गृहीत्वानिच्छतीं स्त्रियम् । केशबन्ध उपानीय बाहुभ्यां परिषस्वजे ॥ २८ ॥ सोपगूढा भगवता करिणा करिणी यथा । इतस्तत: प्रसर्पन्ती विप्रकीर्णशिरोरुहा ॥ २९ ॥ आत्मानं मोचयित्वाङ्ग सुरर्षभभुजान्तरात् । प्राद्रवत्सा पृथुश्रोणी माया देवविनिर्मिता ॥ ३० ॥ तस्यासौ पदवीं रुद्रो विष्णोरद्भुतकर्मण: प्रत्यपद्यत कामेन वैरिणेव विनिर्जित: ॥ ३१ ॥ तस्यानुधावतो रेतश्चस्कन्दामोघरेतस: शुष्मिणो यूथपस्येव वासितामनुधावत: ॥ ३२ ॥ यत्र यत्रापतन्मह्यां रेतस्तस्य महात्मन: तानि रूप्यस्य हेम्नश्च क्षेत्राण्यासन्महीपते ॥ ३३ ॥

srimadbhagavatam.org Translation

The completely naked woman saw him coming and most embarrassed with a smile ran here and there to hide behind the trees. Lord S'iva, Bhava, distracted by his senses, fell victim to lust like he was a male elephant going for a she-elephant. Speeding after Her, he caught Her by the braid of Her hair and pulled Her close against Her will in order to embrace Her.

Vedabase Translation

The beautiful woman was already naked, and when She saw Lord Śiva coming toward Her, She became extremely bashful. His senses being agitated, Lord Śiva, victimized by lusty desires, began to follow Her. After following Her with great speed, Lord Śiva caught Her by the braid of Her hair and dragged Her near him. Although She was unwilling, he embraced Her with his arms.

SB 9.14.4 (Soma kidnaps Tara)

सोऽयजद् राजसूयेन विजित्य भुवनत्रयम् । पत्नीं बृहस्पतेर्दर्पात् तारां नामाहरद् बलात् ॥ ४ ॥

srimadbhagavatam.org: After he had conquered the three worlds, he performed a Râjasûya sacrifice and kidnapped in his arrogance with force Târâ, the wife of Brihaspati.

Vedabase: After conquering the three worlds, Soma, the moon-god, forcibly kidnapped Bṛhaspati’s wife, whose name was Tārā.

SB 9.20.36 (Brihaspati and Mamata)

अन्तर्वत्‍न्यां भ्रातृपत्‍न्यां मैथुनाय बृहस्पति: प्रवृत्तो वारितो गर्भं शप्‍त्वा वीर्यमुपासृजत् ॥ ३६ ॥

srimadbhagavatam.org: Brihaspati felt attracted to his brother’s pregnant wife and wanted to make love to her. But when the son in the womb forbade him, he cursed him and discharged his semen anyway.

Vedabase: When Bṛhaspati was attracted by his brother’s wife, Mamatā, who was pregnant, he desired to have sexual relations with her. The son within her womb forbade this, but Bṛhaspati cursed him and forcibly discharged semen into the womb.

SB 9.24.33 to 35 (Surya and Kunti)

तदैवोपागतं देवं वीक्ष्य विस्मितमानसा । प्रत्ययार्थं प्रयुक्ता मे याहि देव क्षमस्व मे ॥ ३३ ॥ अमोघं देवसन्दर्शमादधे त्वयि चात्मजम् । योनिर्यथा न दुष्येत कर्ताहं ते सुमध्यमे ॥ ३४ ॥ इति तस्यां स आधाय गर्भं सूर्यो दिवं गत: सद्य: कुमार: सञ्जज्ञे द्वितीय इव भास्कर: ॥ ३५ ॥

srimadbhagavatam.org: When she saw the godhead appearing before her, she asked him to return and excuse her. The sun god then made her pregnant and returned to his heavenly abode.

Vedabase: She told the sun-god that she had called him unnecessarily and asked him to return. The sun-god said their meeting could not be fruitless and then discharged semen into her womb.

Devi Bhagavata Purana

sacred-texts.com

DBP 2.6.13 to 35 (Surya and Kunti)

Sûta then said: -- “O Dvija! While Sûrasena's daughter Kunti was a virgin girl... Durvâsâ Muni gave her a mantra... Seeing the God Sûrya had arisen in the sky, Kunti uttered the Mantra... The Sun, then, assuming an excellent human form, came down... Sûrya Deva said: -- 'O Kunti! ... Seeing you, I have become passionate; so come to me. By means of the mantra, you have made me your subservient so take me for intercourse.' Hearing this, Kunti said: 'O Witness of all! O knower of Dharma! You know that I am a virgin girl. I bow down to you; I am a family daughter; so do not speak ill to me.' Sûrya then said: 'If I go away in vain, I will be an object of great shame... If you do not satisfy me, I will immediately curse you and the Brâhmin who has given you this mantra.' Thus saying Sûrya Deva enjoyed the bashful Kunti... The beautiful Kunti became pregnant...”

Skanda Purana

wisdomlib.org

SkP 5.3.136.1 to 16 (Indra and Ahalya)

Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya said: 1-7. Thereafter... Śatakratu, the king of Devas, was infatuated by the exceptional beauty of Ahalyā... 8-16. Sensing her emotional upheaval... he assumed the excellent guise of the sage and carnally delighted Ahalyā who believed that he was Gautama in the inner apartment... Gautama became highly enraged and cursed Devendra.

Brahma Vaivarta Purana

BVP Prakriti Kanda 21.18-24 (Vishnu and Tulsi/Vrinda)

O Narada, thereafter lord Visnu enjoyed the company of the damsel. TulasI felt more delighted than before and she ultimately came to know about the reality and said: “Who are you”? TulasI said: “Are you not the lord of illusion”? You tell me who you are? You have deceitfully enjoyed my company and have defiled my chastity. Therefore I shall pronounce a curse on you. TulasI said: O lord, you are heartless, having no compassion. You are hard like a stone. You have defiled my chastity deceitfully and have killed my husband.

BVP Krishna Janma Kanda 61.43-44 (Indra and Ahalya)

Reaching there she narrated the entire story to the sage Gautama... in the meantime Indra impersonating himself as Gautama enjoyed the company of Ahalya. Gautama spotted Indra moving out of his hermitage and found Ahalya lying there. The sage then pronounced a curse on Indra.

BVP Krishna Janma Kanda 80.11-19 (Soma and Tara)

Tara said: O moon you leave me... I happen to be the wife of your teacher and also the daughter of a Brahmana... I happen to be your mother and you happen to be my son. The moon did not listen to Tara and got ready to cohabit with her... thereafter the moon caught hold of Tara and enjoyed her company while she was crying and sobbing.

Matsya Puranam

MP 49.17-28 (Brihaspati and Mamata)

Suta said: Brihaspati, whilst staying on Earth, one day saw the wife of his brother, Mamata, who was big with child, and addressed her thus: “Dress yourself well and let us enjoy.” She replied that the embryo in her womb was mature and that his proposal was sinful. After saying that, he carried out his desire by force. When the child from her womb cried out, Brihaspati replied in anger and cast his seed on the floor.
